About

Vincent Runge, Assistant Professor of Statistics at Université d’Évry Paris-Saclay.

Vincent Runge

Assistant Professor of Statistics · Université d’Évry Paris-Saclay · LaMME (CNRS UMR 8071)

My research focuses on change-point detection and efficient algorithms for structured data. I work on dynamic programming and pruning methods, extensions to non-Euclidean spaces, and computational geometry for time-series analysis.

GitHub · Google Scholar · Email

Current position

I have been an Assistant Professor in Statistics at Université d’Évry Paris-Saclay since September 2019. I am a member of the LaMME laboratory.

Research interests

  • Change-point detection, dynamic programming and functional pruning
  • Methods for complex data in Hadamard and CAT spaces
  • Computational geometry and time-series compression
  • Constrained and convex optimization
  • Topological Data Analysis

See the research overview and publications.

Supporting Ukrainian Mathematics Students

Since 2022, I have dedicated a significant part of my time to supporting Ukrainian mathematics students in obtaining scholarships, in collaboration with the FMJH through the FMJHcare program and the FSMP. So far, we have helped about 40 students with different programs. I also advise them on finding internships, improving their CVs and motivation letters, and navigating the complex French higher-education system.

🎺 The UMD 2026 conference at IHP and the UMD 2025 day in Évry are examples of this ongoing effort.

Mathematics in Ukraine: continuing traditions

As a side event of the UMD 2026 conference, I proposed and coordinated an exhibition created by my colleagues in Ukraine. It was displayed in the entrance hall of the Henri Poincaré Institute from May to October 2026.

The contributors are:

  • Iryna Banakh, Institute for Applied Problems of Mechanics and Mathematics (Lviv), National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine.
  • Taras Banakh, Viktoriia Brydun, Olena Hryniv, Yaroslav Prytula, and Mykhailo Zarichnyi, Ivan Franko National University of Lviv.

Conception & Graphic Design: Wonjin Park

The exhibition panels are available online in this PDF file. The Poincaré Institute can send the panels to other institutions in France or abroad upon request.

⚠️ Ukrainian students in mathematics are welcome to contact me for advice and support (one easy task is to connect them with other Ukrainian students in a similar situation).
